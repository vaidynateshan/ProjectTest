WhatsApp Messaging Privacy Policy

Samko Holdings Limited · Last updated 6 September 2026

This policy explains how Samko Holdings Limited handles personal data when you message us on WhatsApp. It covers our WhatsApp Business number only. For our other services, see our main privacy policy.

Samko Holdings Limited

We are the data controller for the personal data described below. You can contact us at vaidy@samko-holdings.com.

What we collect

When you send a WhatsApp message to our business number, we receive and store:

DataWhy we hold it
Your message content, including any images, documents or other attachments you send To read and respond to your enquiry
Your WhatsApp phone number To identify the conversation and reply to you
Your WhatsApp profile name, as you have set it To address you correctly
Timestamps and delivery status of messages To keep conversations in order and confirm delivery

We do not collect your WhatsApp profile photo, your contacts, or any message you send to anyone other than us.

Why we process it, and our lawful basis

We process this data to respond to your enquiry and to keep a record of our correspondence with you.

  • Where you are asking about, or already have, a contract with us, our lawful basis is performance of a contract.
  • Otherwise our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries and running our business. You messaged us expecting a reply, and we hold no more than we need to give one.

We do not use your WhatsApp messages for marketing, and we do not use them for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

Who we share it with

Meta Platforms

Your messages reach us through the WhatsApp Business Platform, operated by Meta Platforms Ireland Limited. Meta processes messages in order to deliver them. WhatsApp's own handling of your data is governed by WhatsApp's privacy policy.

Anthropic

We use Claude, an AI assistant provided by Anthropic, to help us read, search and draft replies to messages. When we do so, the content of the relevant conversation is sent to Anthropic for processing. Anthropic acts as our processor and handles the data under its commercial terms. We use this to answer you more quickly and accurately; a person at Samko Holdings remains responsible for what we send you.

No one else

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with any other third party except where we are required to by law.

Where it is stored

Messages are stored in a database on computing infrastructure controlled by Samko Holdings Limited. Processing by Meta and Anthropic may involve transfers outside the United Kingdom; those transfers rely on the safeguards in those providers' own terms, including standard contractual clauses where applicable.

How long we keep it

We keep WhatsApp conversations for 24 months from the date of the last message in the conversation, after which they are deleted. If a conversation relates to a contract or a legal claim, we may keep it for longer where we are required to.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you have the right to ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct it if it is wrong;
  • delete it;
  • restrict or object to how we use it;
  • provide it in a portable format.

To exercise any of these, email vaidy@samko-holdings.com. We will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Stopping the messages

You can stop messaging us at any time, and you can block our number in WhatsApp. If you would like us to delete the conversation, ask us and we will.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle WhatsApp messages, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
